abrp-mcp

Plan EV road trips by talking to an AI assistant — charging stops, state of charge and timings, powered by A Better Route Planner.

MCP endpoint
https://abrp-mcp.vercel.app/mcp
  • Route planning with charging stops, and multi-day trips
  • Charger search, vehicle models & charge curves
  • Towing, weather and network preferences
  • Live telemetry

Unofficial — not affiliated with Iternio. Bring your own ABRP API key.

No tracking, no analytics, no database — the server is stateless and stores nothing; your key is sealed into your own access token. It runs on Vercel (standard host logs only) and forwards planning requests to ABRP and to whichever AI client you connect. Want it fully private? Self-host and use a local LLM.
